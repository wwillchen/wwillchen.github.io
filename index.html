I am an undergraduate at Duke University. I work on generative models and reasoning.

I am really fortunate to be advised by Alexi Gladstone and Xiang Cheng. In addition, I am grateful to have worked with Zeyu Li and Hun Kang.

Research

I am interested in how generative models learn and how to make them reason. Recently I have worked on flow matching with learned interpolation paths, on scaling continuous diffusion language models to math and code, and on understanding what attention and MLPs do inside diffusion transformers.
